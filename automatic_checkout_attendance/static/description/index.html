Overview

This module is designed to automate and streamline the employee attendance process in Odoo. It ensures that employees are automatically checked out after a specified duration, preventing forgotten checkouts and helping HR maintain accurate records.

Key highlights include automatic checkout after 7 hours if employees forget to check out for lunch, a 30-minute block before they can re-check-in to avoid misuse, and an enforced auto-checkout after 8 total working hours to ensure compliance with working policies.

With this module, attendance is tracked more reliably, errors are minimized, and HR teams save time on manual adjustments.

Features

✔ Auto-checkout after 6 hours for lunch breaks
✔ Enforces 30-minute re-check-in block
✔ Auto-checkout after 8 total working hours
✔ Prevents misuse of attendance system
✔ Seamless integration with HR Attendance
